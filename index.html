Every figure is a closed-form result, not a simulation: because each unit of stake is an independent trial, the hit distribution is exact and the final-window share reduces to share = exp(−c · stake) with c fixed by the table's shape. That is why one table covers a stake band rather than a single volume. Observed profiles are the six VA titles launched between 2026-03-05 and 2026-09-03, each stored only as ratios to its own median with the first 21 days of launch excluded, so the page carries volatility shape and no absolute figures. Scale them with the expected-median input.
